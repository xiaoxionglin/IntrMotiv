# IntrMotiv assets for the SAB 2026 talk

Four slide-size cards complement the external 30-minute SAB talk outline and current `SAB2026_ICLR_Expanded_XiaoXiong_Lin_v15.pptx`. The 16:9 deck is 13.33 × 7.5 inches. Each card is 12.2 × 5.5 inches, with editable SVG text and paths, plus a 2196 × 990 PNG preview. Place one card at about 12.2 inches wide below a slide title, or use its own top heading as the slide title. Most labels are at least 14.5 pt; the smallest trajectory colorbar ticks are 13.5 pt, or about 12.2 pt at an 11-inch placement width. The [renderer](render_assets.py), [source manifest](manifest.json), and selected COSYNE [palette manifest](../../07_abstracts/cosyne_2027_submission_draft/figure_palette_candidates/manifest.json) preserve editability and provenance.

The card status is **ongoing IntrMotiv**, separate from the [published *Lin, Yiu & Leibold, ICLR 2026* paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=li1vfqDzRD) and the planned SimGaY experiment. NG, UG, and FG mean no-goal C01, uniform-goal C05, and frontier-goal C15. They use the selected COSYNE slate/olive/rose palette; the word *push* identifies the orange encoder-credit mechanism, while the per-architecture marks retain the architecture hues. Dots show all included models or matched training seeds. Boxes show the interquartile range and median; whiskers show the observed range. They are not confidence intervals, and no significance test is implied.

## Boundaries in the 30-minute narrative

- Slides 2–20 and 24's published-result sentence refer to *Lin et al., ICLR 2026*. For slide 11, the transfer curves after a new reward, blocked route, or map are a **best-performing-seed case study with further training**. For slide 20, permuting outgoing weights of 32 units selected by high spatial information changes one reported navigation comparison; it does not isolate spatial geometry as the cause.
- Slides 21–22 and these cards refer to **ongoing IntrMotiv experiments**. A frozen-weight policy contrast changes sampled experience; a common-observation terminal replay changes the encoder's training history but fixes the evaluation observations. Neither alone establishes transfer after a changed goal. The graph backup measures stored structure and detector events, not physical goal completion.
- Slide 23 presents **planned SimGaY hidden reward blocks** and a proposed post-switch choice measure. It has no result card here.

## Design references and reusable lesson

The primary-source examples informed sequence and readability only; no external figure or data was copied. [Gershman's “Predictive Maps in the Brain”](https://cbmm.mit.edu/video/predictive-maps-brain) starts from the decision problem before introducing a predictive-map account, supporting this talk's task-first opening. [Stachenfeld's “Representations of task structure in hippocampus and entorhinal cortex”](https://neuroscience.stanford.edu/mbct/events/representations-task-structure-hippocampus-and-entorhinal-cortex-kimberly-stachenfeld) distinguishes a representation of topology from its later planning use; the talk should likewise separate DG-map measurements from goal-control evidence. [Google DeepMind's navigation-agent account](https://deepmind.google/blog/navigating-with-grid-like-representations-in-artificial-agents/) places example routes, internal response maps, and a causal manipulation in a readable order; here that suggests route → representation → qualified intervention, without importing its conclusions.
