## Question and matched design

Does the C05 controller family behave differently from the concurrently trained C15 FiLM family when both can pursue a DG goal until a hit or the physical episode ends? This is the intended main C05–C15 family contrast for the COSYNE abstract. The [three-run supplement StudySpec](../../hpc_runs/studies/episode_long_c05_supplement_20261008.study.json) pairs seeds 8, 99 and 123 with the C15 FiLM arm in the [immutable twelve-run episode-long study](episode_long_dg_controller_20261008.md). Compare at the same 75M and 150M frame ages; the latter is a within-episode-long comparison, not a finite-horizon control.

The C05 supplement retains the C15 FiLM run's 16 learned DG units, all 16 eligible goal IDs, fixed visual trunk, CA3 $L=64$, target-ID FiLM worker, reward rule, immediate target timing, reduced action set, monitor-only recruitment, zero replacements, 900-decision episodes, workspace paths and training schedule. The exact rendered argument audit found three scientific differences: C05 uses `visit_direct` in place of `frontier_direct`, DG global punishment $0.01$ in place of $0$, and DG row repulsion $1.0$ in place of $0$. The visit-direct rule favors least-visited eligible identities, with a reachable-candidate preference when available; the frontier manager uses its uncertainty and discovery score. Their DG event-recognition semantics also differ. Thus C05 versus C15 is a **controller-family package contrast**, not an estimate of any single setting's effect. The historical C05 run is a contextual reference, not a matched episode-long control.

## Analysis for the abstract

Use paired seed and age comparisons for online coverage, goal exposure and graph development, but use frozen matched-start **physical arrival under alternative commands** as the primary control measure. Report arrivals at 64, 128 and 256 decisions and at episode end, including failures and censoring, per-goal support, action changes, and time to arrival. Passing through another DG field before the commanded one does not count as failure. The common manifest-driven 10k-decision evaluation supplies field, trajectory and graph summaries at the declared checkpoint ages, with terminal seeds 8 and 123.

The interpretation must preserve the package boundary. A C05–C15 difference could reflect goal selection, DG regularization, recognition, or their interaction. Historical C05 and C15 results are useful motivation but do not isolate the episode-long effect. The abstract can claim a replicated family-level contrast only after the frozen command and spatial evidence is complete; online option success alone is insufficient.

## Reusable lesson

A supplemental condition can share the exact runtime and evaluation schedule with an already submitted study through a separately fingerprinted StudySpec. Comparing rendered arguments before launch keeps the scientific delta explicit while preserving the original submission record.
