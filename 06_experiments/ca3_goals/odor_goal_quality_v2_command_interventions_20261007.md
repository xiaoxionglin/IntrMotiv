# Odor and CA3 goal quality: terminal command interventions

**Status, 8 October 2026:** all 40 frozen 75M target-hit jobs and all 40 first-distinct-outcome jobs completed successfully. Each protocol has 48,000 analyzed trials over the same 40 training checkpoints. **The primary control outcome is whether the requested DG goal is reached within its deadline, even after other DG events.** First-distinct outcome is a secondary diagnostic. The completed jobs score a context-matched shuffled *label* on each executed trajectory; they do not replay an alternative command from the identical environment and recurrent state. The first full-panel exact-start qualification timed out after eight hours without a summary. A bounded four-source replacement qualification completed successfully; it covers one seed and one quarter of the source IDs. The complete 40-run causal analysis remains open.

## Protocols and evidence

Both evaluators start only on an exclusive DG source event and balance five commanded trials over each of 240 off-diagonal directed source/target pairs. The target-hit evaluator stops at the commanded target, a graph-derived deadline, or an episode boundary. The first-distinct evaluator stops at the first new exclusive DG identity, that deadline, or a boundary. Both record observed DG hits and score a nearest-context, same-source shuffled target label chosen from another trial. **The shuffled command is not executed.** Policy, DG, and graph buffers remain frozen. Each of the 80 outputs contains 1,200 trials and all 240 complete pairs. Every target-hit summary carries the original workflow 1.14 StudySpec SHA-256 `c325acb58098b763c5a8e7c0d97a07bca676eaa8dde4e262b6c9dffc4a94d7de`; each first-distinct summary carries the evaluation-only SHA-256 `602ea435864c7a193d0563c365e406c93be91d80aceb3cfdbc8b5da8be1b8000`.

## First-distinct outcomes

The target-hit trial **ends when its commanded identity is reached**. Its shuffled target is checked only against DG events seen before that stop. A commanded target can therefore appear to beat the shuffled target because the trial was stopped in its favor, even if its command had little effect on actions. The target-hit CSV records a hit mask, not the order of wrong DG identities, so the first distinct outcome could not be reconstructed afterward. The separately evaluated first-distinct protocol removes that stopping-rule advantage for the *first new event*.

## What these probes cannot establish

The shuffled label is scored on the path generated under the commanded target; it is not a second path produced by executing the shuffled command from exactly the same observation, recurrent state, and environment state. The first-distinct comparison is therefore a command-specificity diagnostic, **not** a measure of eventual goal-hit control and not an exact-start causal treatment effect. A policy can reach the requested goal after visiting other DG identities. The small action-distribution changes and near-reference first-event rates motivate the direct test but cannot negate the observed target-hit rates.

## Reusable lesson

Check that a declared scientific outcome is identifiable from the trial schema **before** launching an evaluation matrix. A target-hit summary and hit mask do not recover first-distinct event order. Also distinguish a shuffled label scored retrospectively on one path from an alternative command actually executed from the same start. The first gap was repaired with a separately fingerprinted evaluation-only StudySpec, exact checkpoint-manifest equality, and no change to the 40 trained agents; the second needs exact-start replay.
