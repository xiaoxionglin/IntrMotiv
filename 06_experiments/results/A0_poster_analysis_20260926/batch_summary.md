## What was completed

Each selected checkpoint has a 10,001-observation frozen-policy DG field, complete trajectory and occupancy, local occupancy flow, and stored graph matrix. The [33-run table](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/batch_per_run.csv) and [stored graph structure](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/stored_graph_main.csv) give all-seed metrics for Saturday, CPU2048, DGP, and the developmental transfer stage. They also retain an older DGC Direct snapshot for dynamics only; the current DGC Direct endpoint is in its [frozen-policy table](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/dgc_direct_latest_frozen_derived.csv), [common-replay table](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/dgc_direct_latest_replay_derived.csv), and [stored-graph table](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/stored_graph_dgc_direct_latest.csv). A complete observation/action-history panel is replayed within each representation comparison; [panel provenance](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/common_panel_inventory.tsv) records occupancy and cue counts. The 75M transfer panel includes all five cues and is identical across D50/D51 source and random conditions.

The latest transfer field tables are [D50 frozen](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/d50_75m_latest_frozen_derived.csv), [D50 replay](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/d50_75m_latest_allcue_replay_derived.csv), [D51 frozen](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/d51_75m_frozen_derived.csv), and [D51 replay](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/d51_75m_allcue_replay_derived.csv). The paired per-cue heldout tables use those same endpoint checkpoints.

DG, full CA3 trace, and decoder-1 population-vector displacement kernels follow the idea in Jannek's `analysis_kernel.ipynb`. The full-range rerun covers all offsets from −18 to +18 bins on each axis (37×37 arrays), including diagonal distances through 25 bins. Self-correlation is masked; all-heading and heading-matched versions are saved. The saved DG 19×19 rate maps and occupancy suffice to recalculate DG kernels directly; a checked CPU2048 run reproduced the model-replay DG value and pair count exactly at a one-bin offset. CA3 and decoder-1 aligned activity was not saved, so their kernels required forwarding the existing observation/action histories through the same checkpoints, without new DMLab trajectories. For transfer, kernels are also estimated separately for each cue and then averaged with equal cue weight. These are representation measurements on a common history. Frozen-policy trajectories, occupancy, flow, graph buffers, command interventions, and heldout reward trials are separate behavior measurements.

The five-cue heldout evaluator uses 40 reset seeds per training seed, a 1,800-decision horizon, and the same requested reset, cue, engine seed, start, and reward center within each source/random pair. The [paired-trial analyzer](../../analyze_poster_heldout.py) rejects unmatched starts before calculating physical success and horizon-censored time.

In the transfer comparison, `SOURCE_DG` uses the frozen source representation; `RAND_DG` is the calibrated random control, with its BatchNorm moments set by one unlabeled minibatch. Both arms retain their trained downstream policies at each checkpoint.

## Main quantitative findings

- **Saturday:** On common history, ARR has lower active-only DG map cosine than SRC in all three paired seeds (means 0.079 versus 0.115). Frozen-policy means are 0.081 versus 0.178. SRC visits 80.2% of coarse cells versus ARR's 75.7%, but this coverage ordering is not shared by every seed. In bounded command probes, ARR executed versus shuffled target success is 0.632 versus 0.425; SRC is 0.620 versus 0.399. Complete ordered-pair coverage is only 1.9% and 8.1%, respectively.
- **CPU2048:** At the actual six-run 300M shared checkpoint, Waypoint F64 has more distinct DG peak bins than Direct F16 (33.3 versus 15.3 per seed) and lower common-history map cosine (0.145 versus 0.177). The checkpoint-stored reliable graph has 1.0% versus 93.8% reachable ordered node pairs, respectively. In bounded command probes, Direct executed versus shuffled target success is 0.464 versus 0.358; Waypoint is 0.211 versus 0.185, with fewer than 1% of ordered pairs complete in either arm. This bundles capacity, manager, and goal-interface differences.
- **DGP:** HIT has 96.1% stored-graph reachable ordered pairs versus FIRST's 4.9%. Their common-history DG map cosines are similar (0.067 versus 0.071). In bounded frozen command probes, executed versus matched shuffled target success is 0.563 versus 0.411 for HIT and 0.679 versus 0.434 for FIRST. Complete pair coverage is 78.5% and 35.7%, respectively; these rates do not establish that either graph is prospectively correct for every pair.
- **DGC:** At its exact three-seed 300,023,808-frame endpoint, Direct-Worker F16 has 16 active DG units, 0.132 active-only frozen-policy map cosine, 50.3% coarse-cell visitation, and 95.8% stored-graph reachable pairs. On the same replay panel, its map cosine is 0.088. Its bounded command probe yields 0.662 executed versus 0.451 matched-shuffled target success, with only 1.4% complete ordered-pair coverage. The Waypoint F64 candidate seeds have 63–64 active units and 0.071–0.105 frozen-policy map cosine; their individual graph reachability is 2.2%, 9.0%, and 15.5%. Their replay map cosines are 0.065–0.081. An [exact-start bounded command probe](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/dgc_waypoint_command.csv) covers only two of 64 source nodes per Waypoint seed and gives arrival lift 0.000, −0.125, and +0.125; it cannot support a general control claim. The two command probes use different protocols. Waypoint checkpoints span 135–198M frames, so these are descriptive candidates rather than a matched-age contrast.
- **Transfer reward:** Canonical 0–75M scalar histories show lower source-DG reward AUC in **all six** paired seed comparisons. The mean source-minus-random reward per step is −0.000074 for D50 and −0.000058 for D51; terminal 65–75M reward remains lower (D50 0.000176 versus 0.000266; D51 0.000129 versus 0.000309). Early 0–10M and 0–20M differences are mixed, so 50M is only a learning-dynamics snapshot.
- **D50 transfer representation and behavior:** On the all-five-cue common history, source DG has higher mean active-unit spatial information than random DG (0.062 versus 0.009 bits), lower active-only map cosine (0.040 versus 0.058), and fewer active units (54 versus 63.7). Mean equal-cue far-offset population correlation is 0.216 versus 0.077 in DG and 0.195 versus 0.048 in CA3, then reverses to 0.447 versus 0.630 in decoder-1; all three seed pairs have the decoder-1 reversal. Under their own frozen policies, source visits 45.3% of coarse cells versus random's 80.5%, lower in all three paired seeds. The stored graph reaches 5.3% versus 10.3% of ordered node pairs. These are descriptive associations, not a mediation test.
- **D50 heldout control:** At 75,022,336 frames, source succeeds on 60/120 exact matched-reset trials (50.0%) versus random's 70/120 (58.3%). Source-minus-random seed effects are −25.0, −7.5, and +7.5 percentage points, so the overall deficit is not uniform across seeds. By cue, source is 15/36 versus random's 22/36 on cue 4 and 6/18 versus 12/18 on cue 5; cue 1 has only six pairs and favors source. The horizon-censored mean is 1,148 versus 1,126 decisions.
- **D51 transfer representation:** On the all-five-cue common history, source DG has higher mean active-unit spatial information (0.043 versus 0.009 bits) but slightly *higher* active-only map cosine (0.059 versus 0.053) and fewer active units (43 versus 64). The source-versus-random difference persists through CA3 and decoder-1: equal-cue far-offset population correlations are 0.637 versus 0.082 in DG, 0.618 versus 0.054 in CA3, and 0.811 versus 0.589 in decoder-1. All three seed pairs retain the decoder-1 ordering. Higher long-range correlation is a population-similarity difference, not by itself better spatial discrimination.
- **D51 heldout control:** At 75,038,720 frames, source succeeds on 57/120 matched-reset cue trials (47.5%) versus random's 74/120 (61.7%); source is lower in each training seed (−22.5, −10.0, and −10.0 percentage points). Cue 4 is 15/36 versus 23/36, and cue 5 is 9/18 versus 16/18; cue 1 has only six paired resets. Source has a longer horizon-censored mean time to termination (1,222 versus 1,057 decisions). These are paired frozen-policy observations, not proof of a causal pathway from the measured kernels.
- **Developmental transience:** The older, exactly paired 50,003,968-frame heldout snapshot gives D50 source/random success of 59.2%/58.3% and D51 of 65.8%/66.7% over the same 40-reset protocol. D51's source disadvantage emerges clearly by its 75M endpoint. The 50M values are used only to show this change over training.

Both 75M transfer endpoints and all priority command probes are complete. The heldout pattern favors calibrated random DG overall in D50 and D51, while the D50 seed effects and cue effects are mixed; the source-to-random representation differences also change direction between D50 and D51 in decoder-1.

The [layerwise kernel table](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/layerwise_kernel_summary.csv) gives the three-seed mean of correlation at one-bin cardinal offsets minus correlation at **six-bin cardinal offsets** on each common history; “far” in this legacy table is a fixed six-bin reference, not the map boundary. The [full-range band table](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/fullrange_summary/fullrange_bands_by_condition.csv) reports all distances and support, with [per-run values](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/fullrange_summary/fullrange_bands_per_run.csv). For example, Saturday ARR/SRC are 0.077/0.044 in DG, 0.272/0.310 in CA3, and 0.054/0.052 in decoder-1; CPU2048 Direct/Waypoint are 0.178/0.190, 0.244/0.261, and 0.026/0.027, respectively. The layerwise ordering is mixed, so DG separation alone does not establish a larger decoder-level spatial contrast.

**Graph meaning:** Frozen archives contain checkpoint-stored `control_edge_confidence` and `control_attempts`, so the matrix color is *stored edge confidence / attempts*. They do not contain fresh prospective success/attempt counters. Reliable graph edges and reachability are canonical thresholded diagnostics of that same stored buffer. Very few nodes satisfy the strict mono-field rule for physical placement; the [spatial drawings](diagnostics/grounded_graphs/) expose this limitation rather than assigning ambiguous DG units invented positions. Command interventions are the separate test of commanded versus shuffled targets.

The [command probe table](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/control_interventions_summary.csv) reports executed and matched-shuffled success, initial-action sensitivity, and complete ordered-pair coverage for Saturday, CPU2048, DGP, and latest DGC Direct. Low coverage is explicit; the table should not be read as an exhaustive graph-control evaluation.

## Reproducibility and workflow lesson

The full lightweight table and figure package is in [poster data](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/) and this report folder. The [analysis metadata](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/study_manifest.json), [full-range kernel metadata](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/fullrange_plan/kernel_fullrange_manifest.json), and [reward collector manifest](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/reward_online_75m_manifest.json) retain schema, workflow version, and source fingerprints. Lightweight [full-range kernel arrays](../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/fullrange_kernels/results/) are copied locally. Bulk trial rows, evaluator caches, and Slurm logs remain under `/work/classic/fr_xl1014-corridor-geometry/IntrMotiv/SF_hipposlam/train_dir/analysis/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/`. The evaluator was run with ordinary manifest rows after print-only review. The canonical `summarize_place_fields.py` and `analyze_place_field_manifest.py` supplied DG metrics; small adapters here added common-history layers, cue equal-weight summaries, flow, and honest graph labels.

What worked was reusing selected immutable checkpoints, canonical manifests, common complete histories, and the process-backed scalar exporter. Two avoidable delays were hardcoded historical workspace roots in evaluator entry points and a milestone-only inventory that missed later direct checkpoints. The isolated evaluator source was repaired, the old-root issue and exact-inventory acceptance criteria are recorded in [infra.md](../../../infra.md), and future batches should scan direct plus milestone files immediately before Slurm submission. The first layerwise kernels saved only a 13×13 window and not the aligned CA3/decoder-1 activity; that forced another model-forward pass for the full range. Saved DG maps already sufficed for DG. Future layerwise analyses should preserve all valid offsets or enough aligned layer statistics for replotting without replay. The 40-reset heldout runs took the longest; submit those alongside representation jobs and set `PYTHONPATH` to the selected isolated source when running local render adapters on NEMO2.
