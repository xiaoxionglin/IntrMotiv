# Historical place-field exemplar extension

The [run index](run_index.md) links **42 run-protocol analyses** to DG maps, mono-field-only aggregated peaks, trajectory and occupancy, and occupancy flow. Fifteen saved online runs with control buffers also have stored-transition, prospective-transition, and source-command diagnostics. Twelve CPD frozen probes have checkpoint-stored graph and issued-goal diagnostics. The [per-run inventory](historical_exemplar_inventory.csv) contains exact counts and spatial metrics; [CPD online](aggregate_cpd_online.svg), [CPD frozen](aggregate_cpd_frozen.svg), [Navigation8](aggregate_n8_online.svg), and [corrected-core frozen](aggregate_ccr_frozen.svg) plots keep each protocol separate. The [online mono-field versus graph scatter](historical_mono_graph_scatter.svg) shows seed variation behind the bar means.

## Checkpoint and observation-age audit

The [checkpoint inventory](../../../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/historical_extension/historical_checkpoint_inventory.tsv) enumerates retained model files for every selected condition and seed. The latest exact common model checkpoint is **75,038,720 frames** for all four CPD C15 conditions and **100,040,704 frames** for corrected-core C04/C05/C15. The 12 new CPD and nine archived corrected-core frozen probes each use 10k policy decisions from those exact checkpoints. CPD's saved 100k-observation training windows end at **75,005,952 frames**, 32,768 frames before the shared terminal checkpoint; they remain useful as separate developmental samples.

Navigation8 STOP and JOINT have no checkpoint frame number common to all six run files, and the three SCR seeds likewise have no exact common checkpoint. Their latest retained model ages range from 122,798,080 to 132,661,248 frames for STOP/JOINT and from 94,584,832 to 129,007,616 for SCR. The matched **75,005,952-frame online snapshots** are therefore shown explicitly as developmental examples, not as terminal architecture comparisons. No older checkpoint was selected for a matched terminal claim because its mono-field result looked better.

## Main findings

The fraction here is **mono-field DG units divided by all 16 DG units**. The canonical field eligibility and multilevel dominant-component criterion were used for each protocol. The CPD terminal and corrected-core values come from frozen 10k-decision policy probes; CPD developmental and Navigation8 values come from saved 100k-observation online windows. The amplitude-weighted spatial score is the existing project metric and should not be interpreted as bits per activation. Graph reachability for frozen CPD is recomputed from its stored checkpoint buffers using the run's graph thresholds; it is not a prospective frozen-policy success test.

## Full-range common-history kernels

The [21-run kernel manifest](../../../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/historical_extension/kernel_manifest.tsv) fixes the latest exact shared checkpoints and a common 10,001-observation/action-history panel within each family. Every run has a 37×37 displacement kernel for DG, CA3, and decoder-1, reaching $\pm18$ bins on each axis. At 13–18-bin offsets, CPD BASE versus GATE DIR GOAL has three-seed mean DG correlation **0.108 versus 0.384** and CA3 **0.214 versus 0.505**, while decoder-1 remains high at **0.811 versus 0.844**. Corrected-core C05 versus C15 is **0.472 versus 0.132** in DG, **0.603 versus 0.245** in CA3, and **0.870 versus 0.822** in decoder-1. These are population-vector correlations on the fixed replay panel, not individual place-field compactness or a measure of frozen-policy control. ## Availability and interpretation

All 42 run-protocol rows have DG fields, mono peaks, trajectory/occupancy, and flow. The 15 CPD/SCR online snapshots with graph buffers have directed stored and prospective transition matrices plus per-command attempt and conditional-success plots. The 12 CPD frozen probes have stored graph, source-command evidence, and issued-goal allocation; the [canonical stored-graph table](../../../../data/poster_missing_analyses_20260926/historical_extension/frozen_cpd_stored_graph_per_run.csv) lists reliable edges and reachability. Their archives do **not** contain fresh prospective transition counters, so no frozen prospective-success claim is made. Navigation8 reference snapshots and nine corrected-core frozen archives lack graph or command buffers; those fields are blank rather than inferred from trajectories. Online training-window paths are policy-driven behavior data but are not frozen checkpoint evaluations. Saved archives lack aligned CA3 and decoder-1 time series, so layer kernels require replaying the common retained observation/action-history panel through each exact shared checkpoint. Navigation8 cannot enter an exact checkpoint-matched terminal kernel contrast.
