# Easy landmark maze: 75M online place-field and trajectory atlas

These are the six matched seed-99, policy-00 snapshots at target 75M frames (actual 75,005,952). Each snapshot retains 100,000 online behavior samples. The figures use the canonical `segmented-atlas/v1` renderer from the verified workflow 1.14 source. No new rollout was run. Raw NPZs and PDF copies remain in the NEMO2 workspace under `train_dir/analysis/`.

The field pages show all DG units, each scaled to its own maximum. Gray denotes unvisited bins and black denotes walls. Cue markers identify reserved sites in both modes; neutral sites are not visually distinctive to the policy. The trajectory overview shows occupancy and separate saved fragments; fragment color is not elapsed time. Read these with the [production analysis](../../../environments_transfer/easy_landmark_maze_production_analysis_20261006.md), especially its all-unit numeric metrics and frozen 10k probes. Open atlas pages at full size to inspect individual cue markers and units.
