# Episode-long DG goals: field quality, exploration and control

**Result owner, 9 October 2026.** This report compares the orthogonal-FiLM episode-long studies, their finite-horizon references, and the later credit, reward-clock and value-target interventions. It keeps the original corrected-core C01/C05/C15 hit-reward ablation in its [separate report](../reward_credit/corrected_core_distance_reward_ablation_20261009.md), because that experiment uses the earlier architecture and deadline. Three seeds (8, 99, 123) are the unit of replication throughout. Training metrics are behavior-time associations; frozen-policy trials are identified explicitly.

## What the results support

1. **Prescribing four real fields improves exploration, but goal following remains unproven.** Prescribed-minus-learned-4 coverage AUC is positive in every paired seed at both 75M (+22.8/+30.8/+24.2 points) and 150M (+26.4/+31.4/+32.7). At 150M, new frozen 10k paths visit 304/311/314 of 361 bins versus 240/251/189 for learned-4, paired gains of +64/+60/+125. Yet the four fixed fields are rare, and the sole complete prescribed episode-long exact-start panel gives only one-seed field-entry lift of 0.083 by the physical-episode bound, with 205/288 trials censored. [Paired online age plot](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/coverage_75_150m_paired.png) · [150M frozen field plot](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/frozen_150m_fields.png) · [executed-command plot](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/matched_command_lift_75m.png).
2. **Removing the 64-decision goal deadline did not establish control.** The 75M prescribed coverage changes were −15.0/+3.1/+9.9 points by paired seed; learned-4 changed −2.1/−2.8/+7.4. The complete learned-4 executed-command panels show both commanded and alternative commands hitting the learned detector in roughly 92–100% of trials by the 900-decision bound, with paired lift −0.010/−0.010/0.000. This is detector behavior, not a learned physical destination. [Executed-command plot](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/matched_command_lift_75m.png) · [trial denominators](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/matched_command_75m_per_horizon.csv).
3. **The C05 package's exploration advantage at 75M does not persist uniformly to 150M.** C05-minus-C15 coverage is +9.8/+15.4/+14.7 at 75M but −12.3/+2.7/+12.2 at 150M by paired seed. Frozen 150M visited-bin changes are −4/0/+78: the difference is largely seed 123. C05 has 1/16, 6/13 and 0/16 qualifying mono-field rows, also seed-dependent. Its goal-action TV at 150M is 0.002/0.000/0.031. The package does not establish improved field quality or control. [Paired age plot](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/coverage_75_150m_paired.png) · [frozen field plot](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/frozen_150m_fields.png).
4. **The source-command clock and goal-hit value boundary do not show a replicated control rescue.** In the matched 100M longer-credit factorial, source-clock C05 exploration coverage rises by +26.7/+20.1/+4.8 points while logged hit rate falls in all three seeds. For C15, stopping value at the commanded hit under the source clock lowers coverage by −13.1/−28.9/−36.5 points and logged hit rate in all three seeds. It raises frozen spatial information while reducing visited support in all six seed-by-clock pairs; the changed path distribution prevents a pure representation claim. Neither result measures executed-command destination lift. [Paired coverage plot](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/reward_factors_100m_coverage.png) · [C15 field/support plot](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/c15_value_stop_100m_field_tradeoff.png) · [all 36 online rows](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/reward_factor_100m_per_run.csv).
5. **Longer PPO credit improves exploration at 100M, with no matched-start control rescue shown.** Jointly increasing $\gamma$ and GAE $\lambda$ raises coverage AUC by +17.8/+12.1/+12.7 points for prescribed DG and +13.4/+14.9/+7.5 for C15, paired by seed. In frozen 10k paths, C15 visited-bin support rises +12/+66/+96, but mean learned-unit spatial information falls in all three seeds (−0.051/−0.044/−0.075 bits). Prescribed logged hit-rate changes are only +0.26/+0.10/0.00 per 1,000 events, and goal-action TV does not increase consistently. [Paired online plot](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/longer_credit_100m_paired.png) · [paired frozen plot](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/longer_credit_100m_frozen_fields.png).
6. **Graph confidence is not navigation evidence in episode-long mode.** At 150M, C15 stores 187–207 reliable directed pairs of 240 and the four-goal arms store all 12 eligible edges, yet the prospective-success fraction is 1.0 only because unresolved commands ending with the physical episode are omitted from its denominator. The exact-start detector panels above do not show corresponding arrival lift. [150M graph table](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/graph_150m_per_run.csv).

These findings do not support claims that a longer deadline, known field locations, or a dense stored graph *by themselves* make this worker reliably follow a goal. They also do not establish a causal zero-control result for every arm: the fixed-field physical-arrival panel lacks two seeds, the newer 100M variants have no complete matched-command panels, and learned DG detector hits often have no defensible unique physical destination.

## Which contrasts answer which question?

| Question | Matched contrast | Age | What changes |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Does the deadline limit goal following? | Orthogonal-FiLM finite versus episode-long prescribed and learned-4, paired seeds | 75M | Goal expiration only; 64-decision fallback versus until hit or physical episode end |
| Do four known locations help? | Episode-long prescribed versus learned-4 | 75M and 150M | Four fixed goal fields replace the four learned goal rows; both keep 12 learned context rows and four eligible IDs |
| Does the C05 package help? | Episode-long C05 versus C15 | 75M and 150M | Manager choice plus DG global punishment and row repulsion jointly; **not** a single-factor ablation |
| Does longer PPO credit help? | Prescribed/C15 original $\gamma=0.99,\lambda=0.95$ versus $\gamma=0.999,\lambda=0.99$ | 100M | Discount and GAE jointly |
| Does a command-source reward clock help? | Source versus nearest-DG clock, same family/seed and continuing value | 100M | Only distance used for the worker hit bonus |
| Does stopping value at goal hit help? | Stop versus continuing worker value, same family/clock/seed | 100M | Only worker GAE/value boundary; physical episode continues |

The first four orthogonal episode-long arms reached 150M; the longer-credit, source-clock, and value-stop arms reached 100M. No 150M finite-horizon or longer-credit control exists. The separate [zero-initialized first episode-long batch](episode_long_dg_controller_20261008.md) and [C05 supplement](episode_long_c05_supplement_20261008.md) were canceled after early qualification and supply no matched terminal outcome. The finite [zero-$M$ prescribed screen](four_prescribed_dg_controller_20261007.md) is useful for the initialization question but is not a control for the later reward factors.

## 1. Did episode-long goals or known fields produce command-specific arrivals?

**Executed interventions at 75M.** Every retained trial compares commands executed from an exact reconstructed start under a frozen policy and graph. The complete learned-4 panels each contain 32 starts, four discovered source rows, 96 target comparisons and 288 executed rows. At 64 decisions, episode-long learned-4 commanded-minus-alternative detector-hit lift is +0.057/0/+0.062 by seed 8/99/123; by 900 it is −0.010/−0.010/0. Alternatives commonly hit the target without commanding it. The finite learned-4 panels have complete three-seed support but their 256-decision lifts are 0/+0.026/−0.057. The small, mixed lift does not justify a claim that removing the deadline taught command following.

The online 19-by-19 mono-field classifier excludes these four rows because their 40-unit support falls below a bin; its zero prescribed score concerns the learned context rows. The eligible learned-4 goal rows have no stable mono-field structure across the three frozen seeds. Higher prescribed exploration therefore does not mean that the worker can route to the rare narrow fields. ## 2. Did more training consolidate fields, paths or graphs?

At 150M, the retained 100k-observation snapshots cover 72/72 declared arm/seed/age rows. Every terminal arm has 16/16 active DG rows; nearly all snapshots visit 318/361 bins (C15 seed 123 visits 316), making this long-window endpoint coverage nearly saturated. The reset-segment path efficiency remains lower for learned-4 (0.086/0.098/0.079) than prescribed (0.178/0.179/0.245), C15 (0.187/0.138/0.134), and C05 (0.169/0.137/0.266). This measures path straightness, **not progress toward the command**. In a common 10k frozen window for seed 99, prescribed visits 311 bins, learned-4 251, and C15/C05 227 each. The [side-by-side occupancy and first four reset segments](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/frozen_paths_150m_seed99.png) show where those policy paths circulate; stars mark the same four physical reference sites in every panel. They are ordinary trajectories, not commanded-goal routes. [All 150M online spatial summaries](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/online_spatial_through_150m_per_snapshot.csv).

The terminal 145–150M **online coverage AUC** remains higher for prescribed than learned-4 in every seed: 65.5/63.3/73.7 versus 39.1/32.0/41.0. The paired advantage is +26.4/+31.4/+32.7 points, comparable to or larger than at 75M. Common-length **frozen 10k paths at 150M** corroborate the spatial-support difference: prescribed visits 304/311/314 bins versus learned-4 240/251/189, paired +64/+60/+125 of 361. C05 versus C15 changes sign across seeds in online coverage (−12.3/+2.7/+12.2), and its frozen visited-bin differences are −4/0/+78. Thus known fields have a reproducible exploration advantage in this cohort, while C05's earlier advantage is not stable with training age. None of these coverage comparisons measures goal-directed arrival. [Paired 75M/150M online plot](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/coverage_75_150m_paired.png) · [150M frozen comparison](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/frozen_150m_fields.png) · [all twelve frozen field rows](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/frozen_150m_all_arms_per_run.csv).

Active-only map cosine for C05 is 0.201/0.067/0.313, so its ten mono-field rows in one seed do not generalize across seeds. ## 3. Did longer credit, source timing or goal-only value solve sparse hit learning?

The 100M reward comparison holds orthogonal FiLM, episode-long commands, 900-decision physical episodes, the four prescribed field locations, and each family's controller fixed. The full 2×2 clock × value-target cross uses $\gamma=0.999$ and GAE $\lambda=0.99$. The original orthogonal run uses $\gamma=0.99$ and $\lambda=0.95$, so the longer-credit contrast changes both parameters jointly. The value-stop change affects the worker GAE boundary only; it does not reset the environment or manager at a hit.

**The source clock is engaged but often irrelevant for prescribed hits.** In source-clock prescribed continuing runs, the exported 95–100M hit-weighted mean paid rewards are 1.008/1.033/1.008 at mean completed-command durations 331/304/333 decisions. Most hits occur after the 70-decision temporal-bonus support. The old nearest-DG clock can be refreshed by an intervening DG event; its reward therefore measures a different interval. A hit-only ablation of the corrected prescribed source-clock arm would mostly remove a near-zero bonus. C05 source-clock still pays 1.364/1.536/1.558 on average and is a more informative reward target. [Hit-weighted exposure](../reward_credit/results/source_distance_hit_dg_20261009/online_95_100m/hit_weighted_95_100m.csv).

**Exploration and hits move in different directions.** With continuing value, C05 source-minus-nearest coverage AUC is +26.7/+20.1/+4.8 points across paired seeds, while its logged commanded-hit rate drops by 14.2/2.7/3.3 per 1,000 selected-goal events. C15 and prescribed source-clock coverage effects have mixed signs. Under the source clock, stopping value at the hit lowers C15 coverage by 13.1/28.9/36.5 points and hit rate by 15.2/10.3/14.2; C05 and prescribed coverage effects vary by seed. A changed value loss or advantage scale is not a comparable performance endpoint when the return target changes. [Paired-seed coverage figure](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/figures/reward_factors_100m_coverage.png) · [full online factor table](results/episode_long_comparison_20261009/reward_factor_100m_per_run.csv).

**Frozen fields show a C15 specificity–coverage tradeoff, not a control rescue.** All 36 longer-credit factorial cells have 100M 10k frozen field rollouts. Prescribed fixed rows are encountered 5–42 times per 10,001 decisions in the nearest-clock continuing controls and 5–37 in source-clock continuing runs; none of their twelve learned context rows passes the mono-field classifier in any seed. C15 source-clock continuing has 2/16, 0/16 and 0/16 mono-field rows; C15 source-clock value-stop has 0/16 in every seed. Yet C15 *mean active-unit spatial information* rises under value stopping in all six paired seed-by-clock cells (+0.005 to +0.100 bits), and active-only map cosine falls in all six (−0.049 to −0.076). All 16 C15 rows are active in every paired frozen run, so silent-unit entry does not explain these means. Frozen visited support simultaneously falls by 33–86 bins in all six cells. This pattern is compatible with sharper expressed maps at the cost of exploration, but the policies sample different places, so it does not isolate a change in DG tuning on fixed observations. C05 source-clock continuing has 0/16, 5/15 and 0/16 mono-field rows; value-stop has 1/16, 0/16 and 0/16. ## Scientific reading and next decision

The representation and credit bottlenecks interact. A narrow fixed target is unambiguous but supplies very few rewarded hits; episode-long commands allow a long search yet do not create a dense direction signal. Learned DG goals supply many detector events, but broadly distributed or multimodal fields can make a detector hit a poor spatial destination. Orthogonal $M$ gives distinct initial command rows; its 75M parameter separation did not translate into large online action sensitivity. The source-clock correction makes the hit reward logically tied to command age, yet it removes the earlier accidental reward refresh from nearby DG events and leaves prescribed late hits almost flat. Stopping value at a hit makes the critic's target goal-specific. In C15 it reduces coverage and hit exposure while increasing measured frozen spatial specificity, a tradeoff that still lacks a matched-command test.

For the next *control* claim, the minimal decisive experiment is a repaired exact-start evaluator that reports independent physical arrival for the four fixed fields, includes episode-end failures in the denominator, and obtains comparable starts for every seed and arm. It should compare the original and longer-credit prescribed checkpoints at 100M, then the source-clock and value-stop variants, using the declared 64/128/256/900 decision windows. For learned goals, first require a stable physical field map before treating detector hits as navigation. Until that panel exists, favor conclusions about exploration, field exposure and reward mechanics over claims of controllability. The original corrected-core encoder/worker distance ablation remains [its own question](../reward_credit/corrected_core_distance_reward_ablation_20261009.md).

**Practical claim boundary.** The reproducible result suitable for a short abstract is that prescribed spatial landmarks broaden exploration, with paired online and frozen spatial support. The graph and learned-detector results should be described as *diagnostics*, since reliability omits censored commands and learned fields often lack a unique destination. Longer credit is a viable exploration change but is not yet a demonstrated worker-control change; C05's manager/regularizer package lacks a replicated terminal benefit. A control mechanism should be selected by executed-command arrival lift from matched starts, with field exposure reported alongside it.
