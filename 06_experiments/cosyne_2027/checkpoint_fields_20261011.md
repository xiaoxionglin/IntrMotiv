# COSYNE 2027: frozen-checkpoint field comparison

**Outline placement:** [4–5 encoder-credit ablations](03_encoder_credit.md) and [6–7 worker timing and scope](04_worker_bonus_and_scope.md). Those question reports lead with final-size figures; this file retains the full checkpoint tables, factor cells, and source boundaries.

**Status, 11 October 2026:** All 98 declared policy-specific frozen-checkpoint field jobs for the available original fresh runs have finished: 38 terminal checkpoints plus additional 5M, 25M, 50M, and 75M checkpoints for seed 17 in every arm and seed 99 except C15 constant credit. All 38 available terminal checkpoints have also been replayed on **one identical 10,001-observation panel**. Retained 100k-window trajectories cover 190 snapshots from 38 runs, and complete directed-edge tables cover 140 snapshots from the 28 goal-conditioned runs. The two failed original C15 constant-credit seeds 8 and 99 await replacement training, so C15 has three paired terminal seeds. The [policy-specific source tables](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/analysis_manifest.json) contain 98 unique checkpoint rows; the [common-panel source tables](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/common_panel_analysis/analysis_manifest.json) contain 38 terminal rows. Matched-command interventions remain the control gate.

The peak area is the exponential Shannon entropy of equal-mass Gaussian-blurred eligible peak density divided by the shared 242 visited bins. At the 100-unit Gaussian width, the C01 area advantage is positive in all five seeds at 0%, 0.025%, and 0.25% activity cutoffs; four of five remain positive at 1%. At the 0.25% cutoff, Gaussian-density Gini is lower in all five C01 pairs, whereas mean pairwise peak distance is higher in only three; the density measures support a more even spatial spread without requiring every pair of peaks to be farther apart. At that cutoff, 50-unit blur yields four of five positive C01 area pairs, and 100- and 150-unit blurs yield five of five. The individual peak dots and normalized densities use the same occupancy mask and color scale; they do not imply that the 16 unit identities align between independently trained networks.

The saved panel comes from an earlier C15-policy rollout: [panel provenance](../reward_credit/corrected_core_distance_reward_ablation_20261009.md) describes its construction. This removes differences in **evaluation sampling**, but it assesses one policy's state distribution, not every reachable state. Training behavior may still mediate how encoder credit changes the learned weights.

At 100M, temporal minus mean-matched constant **encoder** credit gives:

| Architecture; worker hit bonus | Paired seeds | Active-only map cosine | Mean DG spatial information | Distinct active peak bins | Field-eligible units |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| C01; inapplicable | 5 | −0.061, 5/5 lower | +0.016 bits, 3/5 higher | +0.6, 3/5 higher | −1.0, 5/5 lower |
| C05; temporal | 5 | −0.054, 5/5 lower | +0.024 bits, 4/5 higher | +1.2, 4/5 higher | −0.8, 4/5 lower |
| C05; constant | 5 | −0.085, 5/5 lower | +0.041 bits, 3/5 higher | +1.4, 4/5 higher | −0.6, 4/5 lower |
| C15; temporal | 3 | −0.053, 3/3 lower | −0.011 bits, 1/3 higher | +1.7, 2/3 higher | +0.3, 1/3 higher |

The map-cosine direction is consistent across all 18 paired *condition-seed cells*, with C05 seeds reused across its two worker arms. It measures similarity among active DG unit maps over each rollout's visited support. [Pre-threshold logit-map cosine](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/paired_prethreshold_map_cosine_mean.svg) is lower in all five C01 pairs, all five C05 temporal-hit pairs, three of five C05 constant-hit pairs, and all three C15 pairs. Thus the lower similarity is visible before output thresholding in most, but not every, factor arm.

This policy-specific result associates temporal encoder credit with less map redundancy across all four contrasts, but it is **not** a uniform improvement in spatial information: C01 and C15 signs are mixed, while C05 has a positive mean in both worker arms. [The factorized spatial-information plot](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/factorized_mean_spatial_information_bits.png) displays every arm and seed. [The distinct-peak plot](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/paired_active_unique_peak_bins.svg) is generally positive but less consistent than cosine; [pairwise peak distance](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/paired_active_pairwise_peak_distance_bins.svg) switches sign across the two C05 worker arms. The 10k probes also classify one fewer C01 unit as field-eligible under temporal credit in every pair; [that full factorized plot](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/factorized_field_eligible_units.svg) should accompany any apparent diversity benefit. No terminal 10k probe has an entirely silent DG unit, but [mean active fraction](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/paired_mean_active_fraction.svg) is lower in all five C01 pairs and all three C15 pairs, and [single-field fractions](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/factorized_mono_field_fraction.svg) are near zero in C01 and low elsewhere. Lower cosine is not evidence of compact biological place fields. The retained 100k training snapshots show that the units below a 0.25% practical cutoff are low-firing rather than truly dead at a 0.025% cutoff; see the [cutoff analysis](snapshot_peak_spread_20261011.md#are-the-lost-peaks-practically-silent). **The common panel reverses the C01 field-eligibility comparison and makes its spatial-information gain consistent; the C05 constant-hit cosine effect weakens to 3/5.** This illustrates why policy-specific maps alone cannot carry the causal representation claim.

## Worker timing and learning course

![C05 worker-timing spatial-information contrasts](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/worker_mean_spatial_information_bits.png)

Holding the C05 encoder arm fixed, temporal minus mean-matched constant **worker hit bonus** changes policy-specific terminal spatial information by −0.009 bits for temporal encoder credit (1/5 paired seeds higher) and +0.008 bits for constant credit (2/5 higher). [Worker timing per-seed table](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/paired_worker_fields.csv) and [visited-support plot](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/worker_visited_cell_fraction.svg) show mixed signs. On the common panel, the temporal hit bonus changes spatial information by −0.012 and −0.026 bits in the temporal and constant encoder arms, again without a consistent positive effect; see the [shared-panel worker table](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/common_panel_analysis/paired_worker_fields.csv). The field data therefore do not establish a representational advantage of temporal worker-bonus timing. Behavior and hit-latency benefits require the separate online, trajectory, and matched-command readouts.

The [C05 seed-99 checkpoint course](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/c05_seed99_checkpoint_course.png) and [seed-17 course](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/c05_seed17_checkpoint_course.svg) show the four factorial arms at 5M, 25M, 50M, 75M, and 100M. C01 and C15 courses are also retained as [seed-99](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/c01_seed99_checkpoint_course.svg) and [seed-17](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/figures/c15_seed17_checkpoint_course.svg) examples. These are two learning trajectories, each sampled through its own changing policy and visited bins. They demonstrate that several seed-99 spatial-information and cosine effects develop after 5M, but do not establish a population-level monotonic time course or fixed-observation representational drift.

C05 graph support grows from roughly 32–38 prospectively attempted edges at 5M to 48–52 at 100M, leaving about 188–192 of 240 directed pairs without prospective trials at the endpoint. Its stored reliable graph remains sparse: mean reachable-pair fractions are 0.027–0.054. Thus topology and observed detector outcomes answer different questions. Temporal-minus-constant encoder contrasts in reliable edges and reachability have mixed seed signs in C05 and three paired C15 seeds; no uniform graph advantage follows from the strong C01 field result. Holding the C05 encoder constant, temporal worker bonus raises prospective detector-hit fraction by +0.161 in 5/5 constant-encoder seed pairs, but by +0.055 in only 3/5 temporal-encoder pairs. This is a stored/prospective graph readout, not a frozen executed-command effect.

## Matched-command control gate

The [C05 seed-8 preflight summary](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/control_preflight/output/raw/COS27_C05_TEMPORAL_TEMPORAL__s8__f100007936/intervention_summary.json) verifies a frozen policy and graph, exact start, and nine trial rows from one start; its [trial table](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/control_preflight/output/raw/COS27_C05_TEMPORAL_TEMPORAL__s8__f100007936/intervention_trials.csv) shows negligible initial action change across commands. That one-start result is protocol evidence, not an effect estimate. The 48 already submitted C05/C15 matched-command jobs were running at the 11 October 19:40 Berlin check and had not yet produced trial tables. Their fixed 64/128/256/900-decision windows must be analyzed by paired training seed and checkpoint age when complete, with source/goal exposure, exact-start checks, action change, failures, censoring, and detector-hit timing. The existing trial schema contains physical start and endpoint positions but no independently mapped spatial destination for each DG target; therefore a detector hit cannot presently be called a **physical arrival**. Physical arrival remains an explicit open measurement gate.

## Measurement and claim boundary

- [Policy-specific regeneration code](analyze_checkpoint_fields.py) reads seven canonical `derived_place_field_metrics.csv` files and writes [all checkpoint rows](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/all_checkpoint_fields.csv), [encoder-credit paired rows](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/paired_terminal_fields.csv), [worker-bonus paired rows](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/paired_worker_fields.csv), and summaries. It carries each source SHA-256 in the [analysis manifest](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/analysis_manifest.json). [Common-panel code](analyze_common_panel_fields.py) reuses those pairing and plotting methods on the canonical [38-row field table](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/common_panel/summary/derived_place_field_metrics.csv). [Peak-spread code](analyze_common_panel_peaks.py) uses the evaluator's post-inhibition eligible peak coordinates; its [source manifest](../results/cosyne_2027_checkpoint_fields/common_panel_analysis/npz_source_manifest.csv) hashes every remote NPZ without copying raw arrays into the vault. Source StudySpec schemas, workflow versions, and fingerprints are in the corresponding source [manifest files](../results/cosyne_2027_snapshot_peak_spread/inputs/) and [run index](training_runs.csv).
- Each policy-specific field probe contains 10k decisions; the common panel has 10,001 saved observations. The fixed DG capacity is 16 units; field eligibility requires at least 20 active observations in at least three spatial bins. Map cosine is computed only among active unit maps. Spatial information and map shape depend on occupancy. The selected thresholded and pre-threshold source maps are diagnostics; the numerical comparisons use all available paired seeds.
- The [100k-window Gaussian peak-area analysis](snapshot_peak_spread_20261011.md) independently finds broader eligible-peak coverage under temporal credit in all five C01 pairs at a 0.25% activity cutoff, but C05 is conditional on worker arm. Its common pooled visited-bin mask is a shared spatial domain, not a common observation sequence.
- [Graph and trajectory regeneration code](analyze_graph_trajectories.py) uses canonical `collect-spatial --include-details` outputs and validates 240 directed nonself edges at each of 140 goal-run snapshots. It leaves graph-edge tables on NEMO2 and exports factorized per-age tables, paired seed contrasts, and selected plots. The retained 100k-window paths are policy sampled; the atlas preserves reset and segment boundaries. No graph metric, even a prospective detector-hit ratio, substitutes for the running matched-command intervention.

**Current COSYNE sentence:** “In a reward-free visual exploration model, temporally weighted self-supervised encoder credit yields more spatially informative, less redundant, and more spatially distributed DG activity than mean-matched constant credit across five matched C01 seeds, when both networks are evaluated on identical observations.” C05 with a temporal worker hit bonus shows a related but less uniform pattern; the C05 constant-hit arm does not. The common panel removes evaluation-trajectory differences, while still sampling one C15-policy state distribution and allowing training behavior to mediate the total credit effect. The initial attempt to record a new panel failed on an obsolete workspace path; its [failure log](../results/cosyne_2027_snapshot_peak_spread/common_panel_preflight_8331634.log) is archived. Reusing the validated existing panel completed the comparison without changing the training runs.

**Reusable lesson:** Keeping canonical checkpoint CSVs keyed by condition, seed, and target frames made the seven studies join without parsing run names as metadata. Reusing one archived panel and verifying identical occupancy across every NPZ resolved the largest interpretive ambiguity in the earlier policy-specific plots. The canonical retained snapshots already held every graph edge and segmented trajectory, so source-validated CSV aggregation and selected atlas copies finished those components without another DMLab rollout. The remaining bottleneck is executed command control and a spatial destination mapping for physical arrival. Run the canonical postprocessor after each intervention manifest is complete, then add paired command results to this same report.
