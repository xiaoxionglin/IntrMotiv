# Recovery of two corrected-core C15 failures

The original hit-only C15 seed 99 and later-wave zero-bonus, constant-credit C15 seed 123 runs failed near 0.5M frames. Their original jobs, 8317714 and 8322468, were cancelled after the learners stopped. They have no 100M outcome and remain excluded from the existing effect estimates until their replacements finish.

## Diagnosis

Both logs show the same exception: `Learner update invariant violated: fewer than two valid decisions reached PPO`. DG recruitment invalidates rollouts collected with an older representation. The learner's generation barrier accepted a batch with at least 2,048 fresh decisions, but the configured two unshuffled PPO minibatches could place the fresh decisions in one half and leave the other half empty. The batch-level check therefore did not guarantee the per-minibatch requirement needed for advantage normalization.

The [isolated source patch](../../hpc_runs/patches/recover_empty_ppo_minibatches_20261010.patch) skips a PPO minibatch containing fewer than two valid decisions. It keeps the invariant in the loss calculation and leaves valid minibatches trainable. If no minibatch is trainable, it avoids an optimizer step and DG recruitment for that update. The focused NEMO2 tests passed 22/22, including the observed 2,048-valid/2,048-invalid split. The original source and completed runs were not modified.
