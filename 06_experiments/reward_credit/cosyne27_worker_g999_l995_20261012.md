## Question and contrast

At $\gamma=0.999$ and GAE $\lambda=0.995$, does the *timing dependence* of the worker's DG-detector-hit reward matter? Within C05 and C15, hold temporal DG encoder credit, PPO-to-DG gradient stopping, architecture, manager, environment, optimizer, and seeds fixed. Compare the nearest-preceding-DG temporal worker bonus with a family-specific mean-matched constant bonus. Both worker arms retain the base hit reward. This is a timing ablation, not removal of the hit bonus and not a source-command-clock comparison.

C01 has no commanded worker hit reward. Its single temporal-encoder arm measures the effect of the longer joint PPO credit regimen in an architecture without this worker factor. Historical C01 used $\gamma=0.99$ and the runtime default GAE $\lambda=0.95$, so its between-regimen comparison changes both values. It does not estimate the worker-timing contrast. Five training seeds, 8/99/123/17/41, are declared in each production StudySpec; the seed is the paired unit of inference for C05 and C15.

## Required readouts

At 5M, 25M, 50M, 75M, and 100M, compare paired training-seed DG place fields, silent units, active-only map cosine, peak diversity, spatial information, pre-threshold maps, trajectories and occupied support, and stored directed-graph support. At 100M, replay trained models on identical observations to separate representation differences from policy sampling; include CA3 and decoder spatial information. Use the 90–100M scalar histories for hit counts, hit-count-weighted elapsed decisions and reward magnitude, and exploration. Analyze C01 as a horizon control, not as a worker contrast.

For C05/C15 commanded-goal control, keep stored prospective detector hits separate from executed matched-start commands and independently mapped physical arrival. Report command horizons and censoring explicitly. If the evaluator cannot supply complete physical-arrival trials, leave that outcome open rather than treating online detector hits or graph reachability as successful navigation.
