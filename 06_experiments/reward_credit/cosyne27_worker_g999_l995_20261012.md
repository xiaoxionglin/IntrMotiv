## Question and contrast

At $\gamma=0.999$ and GAE $\lambda=0.995$, does the *timing dependence* of the worker's DG-detector-hit reward matter? Within C05 and C15, hold temporal DG encoder credit, PPO-to-DG gradient stopping, architecture, manager, environment, optimizer, and seeds fixed. Compare the nearest-preceding-DG temporal worker bonus with a family-specific mean-matched constant bonus. Both worker arms retain the base hit reward. This is a timing ablation, not removal of the hit bonus and not a source-command-clock comparison.

C01 has no commanded worker hit reward. Its single temporal-encoder arm measures the effect of the longer joint PPO credit regimen in an architecture without this worker factor. Historical C01 used $\gamma=0.99$ and the runtime default GAE $\lambda=0.95$, so its between-regimen comparison changes both values. It does not estimate the worker-timing contrast. Five training seeds, 8/99/123/17/41, are declared in each production StudySpec; the seed is the paired unit of inference for C05 and C15.

For C05, the spatial-information difference stays near zero at all five checkpoints; 100M seed differences are mixed (-0.0116, +0.0219, -0.0883, +0.0444, +0.0210). Its coverage AUC gain is carried by four seeds, especially 123 and 41. The temporal arm has more correct-hit observations in those same four seeds, but the elapsed-on-hit contrast does not indicate faster hits. C05's graph remains sparse in both arms: 2.2 versus 3.0 reliable edges out of 240 possible directed edges at 100M.

For C15, the mean temporal-minus-constant spatial-information differences at 5M, 25M, 50M, 75M, and 100M are +0.0033, +0.0016, +0.0018, -0.0122, and -0.0172. The late decrease is present in four of five terminal seed pairs. It does not accompany more silent DG units: both arms have zero silent units in these snapshots, nearly the same active-only map cosine (0.287 versus 0.270, mixed by seed), and similar unique active peak bins (14.8 versus 14.6 of 16). Both arms visit about 0.88 of the map's grid cells in the 100M windows, near the available-support ceiling, so that terminal support measure cannot explain the information difference by itself. Their path-efficiency differences are mixed by seed.

C15's temporal arm stores more reliable graph edges in all five pairs, but prospective success fractions are similar (0.608 versus 0.616). Temporal has prospective attempts on all 240 directed pairs in every seed; constant averages 231 of 240 because one seed has fewer attempted pairs. Physical endpoint validity and graph-grounded controllability are zero in both arms. Thus the extra stored edges do not demonstrate better physical goal control. C05's prospective success fractions are also similar (0.528 versus 0.534). These are graph observations, not executed matched-start command trials.

The [C05](results/cosyne27_worker_g999_l995_20261012/c05_worker_hit_weighted_90_100m.csv) and [C15](results/cosyne27_worker_g999_l995_20261012/c15_worker_hit_weighted_90_100m.csv) hit tables join each conditional mean to its same-step correct-hit count before averaging, excluding zero-hit updates. Counts are repeated learner-batch observations, not independent physical arrivals. The temporal arms' observed correct-hit reward magnitudes average 1.633 versus 1.592 in C05 (+2.6%) and 1.671 versus 1.590 in C15 (+5.1%). Calibration at 2M therefore did not perfectly match terminal reward amplitude; timing and realized magnitude remain partly confounded. Exact paired sign-flip two-sided $p$ values for the exploratory C05 coverage, C15 spatial-information, and C15 reliable-edge contrasts are 0.125, 0.1875, and 0.0625 before any multiple-comparison adjustment. Five seeds support a directional reading, not a decisive significance claim.

**Interim interpretation:** The timing dependence appears to affect C05 exploration exposure and C15's late own-policy spatial information and stored graph topology. It does not show a consistent improvement in hit speed, a general improvement in spatial representation, or proven goal control. These 100M spatial windows follow each learned policy; fixed-observation replay is needed to separate representation changes from policy sampling. The online snapshots also lack pre-threshold DG maps, so they cannot finish the standard frozen place-field analysis.
