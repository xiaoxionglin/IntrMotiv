# What does distance reward do in the corrected core?

**Result in progress, 10 October 2026.** The [worker hit-bonus ablation](corrected_core_distance_reward_ablation_20261009.md) and 44/45 completed runs of the encoder/worker cross have online, 10k-decision frozen-policy, common-observation place-field, trajectory, and stored-graph analyses. The exact-start executed-command sweep is running. Its result is required before judging goal controllability. C15 zero-bonus, constant-credit seed 123 failed before 5M and has no efficacy result; the first-wave C15 hit-only seed 99 is also missing. The clearest current finding is that generic positive encoder event credit sustains DG response strength; the data do not yet show that temporal distance information is necessary or that stronger responses improve goal following.

## Design and interpretation limits

The [immutable 45-run StudySpec](../../hpc_runs/studies/corrected_core_encoder_worker_later_20261009.study.json) has schema `intrmotiv/study/v1`, workflow `1.14.1`, and SHA-256 `37733408996f10975eb055ea93226885e15bdb36b39e6ee5722e8afad354a853`. C01 has a flat exploration reward and no commanded-hit reward. C05 and C15 cross three encoder settings with worker temporal bonus on/off, each at seeds 8, 99, and 123. The encoder settings are `CONSTANT_STOP` (same qualifying events with a fixed positive mean-matched credit), `NONE_STOP` (no interval credit, policy gradient stopped at DG), and `NONE_JOINT` (no interval credit, policy gradient enters DG). All retain DG maintenance losses. The [first wave](corrected_core_distance_reward_ablation_20261009.md) supplies temporal encoder credit with zero worker bonus; archived September runs supply temporal credit with temporal worker bonus. The first and later waves share the pinned new source. Comparisons with September remain cross-source and have residual source uncertainty.

The constant credit was set before outcomes using the qualifying-event-count-weighted first-5M temporal mean in each archived family. The C01/C05/C15 constants are 3.008, 3.185, and 3.482 distance units; [the calibration record](results/corrected_core_hit_only_20261009/encoder_constant_calibration.json) gives the full values and event counts. Worker temporal bonus versus zero also changes added reward magnitude; it cannot isolate temporal information without a magnitude-matched constant-hit control. Learned C05/C15 DG detector activations are not by themselves arrivals at physical destinations.

The 5M health check used 1–5M frames; subsequent online summaries used the preceding 10M frames at 25M, 50M, 75M, and 100M. All 44 completed runs had finite selected policy/value losses and advantage statistics, observed DG events, and their declared 5M and 100M checkpoints. Every C05/C15 condition had some correct-command outcomes by 5M, though exposure varied greatly. The [220 run-by-age rows](results/corrected_core_encoder_worker_20261010/online_milestones.csv), [terminal 90–100M table](results/corrected_core_encoder_worker_20261010/online_terminal_90_100m.csv), and [cached-history summarizer](summarize_distance_ablation_milestones.py) preserve the numerical checks. The missing C15 seed-123 cell failed near 0.5M with the learner's fewer-than-two-valid-decisions invariant. It was cancelled after its workers stopped, never counted as a zero outcome. No new training qualification was run for the 15 later-wave settings before release; the frozen evaluator successfully loaded their completed checkpoints.

## Encoder reward: fields and exposure

At 100M, removing encoder event credit while blocking PPO-to-DG gradients sharply lowers DG event density and active-unit spatial score in the 100k-observation training snapshots. In the independent 10k-decision frozen rollouts, C01/C05/C15 `NONE_STOP` units often remain technically active but fire rarely. For example, C05 temporal-bonus seed 99 moves from mean active fraction 0.030 and spatial score 0.107 with constant credit to 0.007 and 0.004 without credit. Its 100M graph changes from 5 reliable edges to 0. The [online spatial/graph table](results/corrected_core_encoder_worker_20261010/online_spatial_graph100.csv) records every seed and condition.

We replayed all 44 terminal checkpoints on **the identical 10,001-observation panel** used for the first wave. The panel file SHA-256 is `14e977ff526c71d04822dde8903e4d1eeda8ed44d16b8eb1a9b7d69b426fc54d`; every replay has the same occupancy bytes and 242/361 visited bins. This separates field expression from differences in each policy's own path. The spatial score in the tables is the evaluator's amplitude-weighted score, despite the historical `bits` column name. A decrease can reflect weaker response amplitude, a shape change, or both; the center maps, active-only cosine, field components, and mono-field criterion address shape separately.

The paired common-panel spatial-score changes for `NONE_STOP − CONSTANT_STOP` are below. Each entry is one seed, in order 8 / 99 / 123; an em dash denotes the failed cell. Across the 14 observed matched cells, 13 changes are negative, but these cells share families and seeds and are not 14 independent replications.

### Does temporal encoder weighting beat mean-matched constant credit?

On the same observation panel, `CONSTANT_STOP − historical temporal credit` with the worker bonus present changes the C05 score by −0.036/−0.098/−0.063 across the three seeds. C15 changes by −0.176/+0.015 in the two historical seeds with a common-panel replay. With the worker bonus removed, `CONSTANT_STOP − first-wave temporal credit` changes C05 by −0.041/+0.061/+0.019 and C15 seed 8 by −0.108; the other C15 pairs are missing. Thus C05's temporal-credit advantage under the original worker bonus is a **lead**, but it does not replicate under the hit-only worker and its comparison crosses the lost September source boundary. The hit-only worker comparison shares the pinned new source. These are amplitude-weighted scores, not a demonstrated gain in compactness or control. [The paired table](results/corrected_core_encoder_worker_20261010/common_panel_temporal_vs_constant.csv) preserves each row and its source-compatibility label; the [historical](results/corrected_core_hit_only_20261009/historical_common_panel100.csv), [C05 hit-only](results/corrected_core_hit_only_20261009/c05_common_panel100.csv), and [C15 hit-only](results/corrected_core_hit_only_20261009/c15_common_panel100.csv) tables are the inputs.

## Can PPO gradients replace encoder event credit?

Within the no-event-credit setting, enabling PPO-to-DG gradients partly restores the common-panel spatial score in C01 and C05: `NONE_JOINT − NONE_STOP` is positive in all nine C01/C05 paired cells (+0.016 to +0.059). C15 is mixed (two positive, four negative among six pairs). This is an architecture interaction, not evidence that the joint route makes good fields. Mono-field and peak-diversity changes remain inconsistent. The 100M graph also differs by family: C05 zero-bonus `NONE_STOP` has 0 reliable edges in all three seeds, while `NONE_JOINT` has 11/5/4; C15 zero-bonus `NONE_STOP` retains 114/134/136 reliable edges, while `NONE_JOINT` has 66/90/19. The [common-panel paired table](results/corrected_core_encoder_worker_20261010/common_panel_encoder_pairs.csv) and [online graph paired table](results/corrected_core_encoder_worker_20261010/paired_effects100.csv) contain the individual seed differences.

The directed matrices below visualize **prospective detector hits divided by attempts** from retained 100M graph buffers, with unattempted ordered pairs gray and the same 0–1 scale. Their attempt denominators vary greatly. For seed-99 C05 temporal worker bonus, constant credit has 143,165 total prospective attempts and 5 reliable stored edges; no-credit/STOP has 169 attempts and 0 edges; no-credit/JOINT has 44,904 attempts and 0 edges. JOINT restores C05 exposures without necessarily crossing the reliable-edge threshold. C15 has broader attempts and many stored edges even when its command-related online action sensitivity is only about 0.003–0.012. Stored graph evidence is separate from executed-command success.

## Worker bonus: exploration and trajectories

The [first-wave temporal-encoder comparison](corrected_core_distance_reward_ablation_20261009.md#1-does-removing-the-bonus-change-learning-and-exploration) found C05 terminal online coverage changes of −17.74, −7.80, and −10.34 across seeds 8/99/123 when the temporal hit bonus was removed; C15 was mixed and seed 99 failed. The C05 historical-source uncertainty remains. Within the later wave's intact source, `zero bonus − temporal bonus` coverage changes vary greatly: C05 constant-credit −1.03 / +45.68 / −27.11; C05 no-credit/STOP +4.24 / +13.72 / +16.35; C15 constant-credit +5.51 / −44.86 / missing. Other routes are similarly mixed in the [online paired table](results/corrected_core_encoder_worker_20261010/paired_effects100.csv). This does not support a general coverage benefit for either worker reward.

The [frozen 10k place-field and trajectory table](results/corrected_core_encoder_worker_20261010/frozen_place_fields.csv) separates 44 terminal checkpoints and the seed-99 5M/25M/50M/75M series. At 100M, C01 `NONE_STOP` visits 207/310/281 arena bins versus 182/190/228 under constant credit; C05 zero-bonus `NONE_STOP` visits 297/318/294 versus 255/296/158. More visited bins coexist with weak DG activity and low field score. C05 temporal-bonus and C15 comparisons are mixed. A policy that roams farther is not necessarily a better goal follower or a better field learner.

## Commanded-goal control

The 100M fixed-window matched-command sweep is running for the completed C05/C15 later-wave cells and their available temporal-encoder references. It executes three alternate commands from each verified common start under frozen policy and graph, tracks each target's detector hit through 64/128/256/900 decisions, and includes non-hits and physical episode censoring. C01 has no goal command, so this probe is inapplicable. The declared 75M sweep has not yet been launched. These learned DG goals require independent compact maps before detector hits can be interpreted as physical destination arrival.

A first-wave C05 seed-8 pair did finish with four source IDs, eight repeats each, 32 exact starts, and 288 trial rows per checkpoint. At 64 decisions, commanded and alternative detector-hit rates were both 0.813 without the temporal worker bonus, versus 0.667 and 0.677 in the archived temporal-bonus checkpoint. The 128/256/900-decision commanded-minus-alternative lifts are +0.005/−0.005/−0.005 without the bonus and −0.005/−0.036/−0.005 with it. Initial-action total-variation distance across alternate commands averages 0.00023 without the bonus and 0.0154 with it. The archival policy is more command-sensitive at the first action, but neither seed-8 checkpoint shows a useful detector-hit lift in this bounded sample. Detector hits are frequent even under alternate commands and do not certify destination control.
