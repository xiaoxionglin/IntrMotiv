## Analysis and claim gates

Analyze each age at matched frames and each seed as the paired unit. The representation panel should report DG event exposure, active fraction, spatial score and information on a common observation panel, active-only map cosine, peak diversity with 0.025%/0.25%/1% activity sensitivity, mono-field structure, and decoder-1 information/reliability. The behavior panel should report trajectory coverage, hit-bonus exposure and realized magnitude, graph attempts and retained edges, and executed-command outcomes where applicable. A detector hit is not automatically a physical destination arrival. If an arm receives too few correct hits, the temporal-versus-constant worker result is weakly exercised; adding seeds alone does not establish the intended mechanism.

For the COSYNE draft, positive event credit versus no credit and own-policy versus random-policy decoder-1 expression have stronger existing support. Claims that temporal weighting improves spatial coding or coverage depend on these new same-source comparisons. The separate command-clock bonus by worker-discount study tests faster goal attainment and is outside this submitted matrix.
