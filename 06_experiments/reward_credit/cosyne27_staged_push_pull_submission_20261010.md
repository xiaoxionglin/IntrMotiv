## Completion and C15 recovery

On 11 October, Slurm reported 38 original jobs `COMPLETED` with exit code zero; each had a checkpoint at or beyond 100M frames. The two exceptions, `COS27_C15_CONSTANT_S8` (8327183) and `COS27_C15_CONSTANT_S99` (8327184), stopped at 491,520 and 819,200 learner frames. Their last checkpoints were at 425,984 and 786,432 frames. Both logs show `Learner update invariant violated: fewer than two valid decisions reached PPO`, followed by a missing learner heartbeat. They remained `RUNNING` for about 19 hours without further training and were canceled. Their partial histories are excluded from the planned effect estimates.

The [replacement StudySpec](../../hpc_runs/studies/cosyne27_c15_constant_recovery_20261011.study.json) selects only those two seeds and preserves every scientific command argument. It uses new run names, output roots, and job IDs so the failed and replacement histories cannot be confused. The isolated NEMO2 source is `/work/classic/fr_xl1014-corridor-geometry/IntrMotiv/source_cosyne27_c15_ppo_recovery_20261011/`, copied from the original COSYNE release with the previously qualified [per-minibatch learner patch](../../hpc_runs/patches/recover_empty_ppo_minibatches_20261010.patch), SHA-256 `797dd997ef5d9e83839449fdce8978bfc5b365ee98d47a5d86aecbcea49c93b7`. Its learner, push–pull, and StudySpec tests passed 61/61. The patch skips a PPO minibatch containing fewer than two valid decisions and keeps valid minibatches trainable; this changes behavior only where the original training would have failed. Prior C15 recovery qualifications under this patch reached 2M frames, and the original COSYNE C15 matched-constant qualification reached 2M frames. The two replacement commands passed print-only review, exact scientific-argument parity, and `audit-submission --submitted` using the [archived manifest](results/cosyne27_staged_push_pull_20261010/c15_constant_recovery_20261011_jobs.tsv). ## Analysis and claim gates

Analyze each age at matched frames and each seed as the paired unit. The representation panel should report DG event exposure, active fraction, spatial score and information on a common observation panel, active-only map cosine, peak diversity with 0.025%/0.25%/1% activity sensitivity, mono-field structure, and decoder-1 information/reliability. The behavior panel should report trajectory coverage, hit-bonus exposure and realized magnitude, graph attempts and retained edges, and executed-command outcomes where applicable. A detector hit is not automatically a physical destination arrival. If an arm receives too few correct hits, the temporal-versus-constant worker result is weakly exercised; adding seeds alone does not establish the intended mechanism.

For the COSYNE draft, positive event credit versus no credit and own-policy versus random-policy decoder-1 expression have stronger existing support. Claims that temporal weighting improves spatial coding or coverage depend on these new same-source comparisons. The separate command-clock bonus by worker-discount study tests faster goal attainment and is outside this submitted matrix.
