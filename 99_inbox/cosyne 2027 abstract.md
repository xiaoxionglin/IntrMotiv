How can spatial representation emerge without any spatial supervision?
We designed a system with temporal push pull system inspired by hippocampal DG CA3 physiology without external spatial reward, spatial supervision or explicit spatial objectives.
- spatial information increases with training under this loss function
- representation is shaped by behavior, which is inherently spatial
- temporal push for DG learning increases spatial spread of DG place fields
- temporal pull reward for policy increases spatial coverage of trajectories
