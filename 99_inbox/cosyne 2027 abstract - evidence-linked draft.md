# COSYNE 2027 abstract: evidence-linked bullet draft

[Original outline](cosyne%202027%20abstract.md) · [Run and evidence index](../06_experiments/cosyne_2027/README.md) · [Training-attempt catalog](../06_experiments/cosyne_2027/training_runs.csv)

**Working question:** Can spatially informative activity emerge from visual experience and internally generated behavior without position labels, external task reward, or an explicit spatial representation loss? Which parts of temporal feedback help representation and exploration?

**Proposed story for two letter-size pages:** Lead with the common representation mechanism and the flat C01 model. Introduce C05/C15 goal-conditioned variants as tests of whether the same code can support behavior. Use the fresh five-seed comparisons to decide whether temporal weighting deserves the headline; do not write their direction into the abstract before analysis.

## Abstract bullets, in narrative order

1. **Model and setting.** A fixed ImageNet-pretrained visual trunk feeds a trainable sparse DG-like landmark layer and CA3-like memory. Event-based feedback trains the landmark representation; the policy acts in a reward-free open field. Goal-conditioned worker variants add an internally generated target-hit bonus. This is a computational architecture motivated by hippocampal circuitry, not a claim that the biological circuit implements these exact objectives.
   - **Existing basis:** [Architecture and historical protocol](../06_experiments/corrected_core_reevaluation_20260901.md#shared-configuration), [current training designs and mean-matched constants](../06_experiments/reward_credit/cosyne27_staged_push_pull_submission_20261010.md#scientific-comparisons-and-seed-structure).
   - **Abstract phrasing:** “without spatial labels or extrinsic reward”; avoid “without any spatial objective” if the temporal feedback uses learned spatial transitions.

2. **Spatial coding appears, with a qualified learning-course claim.** DG units and downstream activity show location-dependent responses during reward-free exploration. A strong statement that spatial information rises throughout training needs a matched observation panel: the older checkpoint series is not monotonic across every model and its sampled paths change with policy.
   - **Existing evidence:** [historical field maps and trajectories](../06_experiments/corrected_core_reevaluation_20260901.md), [C05 field course](../06_experiments/reward_credit/results/corrected_core_hit_only_20261009/c05_field_course100.csv), [C15 field course](../06_experiments/reward_credit/results/corrected_core_hit_only_20261009/c15_field_course100.csv).

3. **Behavior changes expression of the learned code.** With network weights frozen, each model's own policy yields more spatial information per activation in decoder layer 1 than uniform random actions in all three C01/C05/C15 three-seed sets. The DG result meets the same predeclared criterion only in C01; other layers and bits-per-step readouts have different patterns.
   - **Existing evidence:** [54-probe intervention report](../06_experiments/dg_representation/behavior_field_expression_20261007.md#results-own-policy-versus-uniform-random), [seed-level figure](../06_experiments/results/behavior_field_expression_20261007/paired_layer_summary.png), [paired measurements and support](../06_experiments/results/behavior_field_expression_20261007/paired_prefixes.csv), [evaluation-to-training-run links](../06_experiments/cosyne_2027/frozen_policy_evaluations.csv).
   - **Interpretation:** This establishes an effect of executed behavior on *expression* of a fixed representation, not that behavior caused the learned weights to develop. The two evaluation seeds are repeats of each training seed.

The no-credit arms can show many distinct peak bins despite weak activity; peak count alone does not establish better fields.

## Likely two-page selection

- **Core results:** One field/trajectory example; the frozen-policy decoder-1 intervention; positive-credit versus no-credit representation health; then the strongest *verified* temporal-versus-constant encoder result.
- **Conditional result:** One compact C05 worker-timing panel only if the five-seed factorial gives a clear coverage or hit-latency effect beyond matched reward magnitude.
- **Context, not headline:** HRL architecture and C15 exploration advantage. A final sentence may state the boundary between spatial coding, exploration, and commanded control.
- **Figure plan:** Model/credit schematic; matched-support fields with activity and information; own-versus-random decoder-1 points; factorized temporal/constant effects with individual paired seeds. Keep graphs and command control in supplementary analysis or text unless they change the central conclusion.
