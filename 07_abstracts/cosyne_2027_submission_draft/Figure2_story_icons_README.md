# Figure 2 story layout: metric-icon variant

[Editable SVG](Figure2_story_icons.svg) · [print PDF](Figure2_story_icons.pdf) · [preview](Figure2_story_icons.png) · [layout manifest](Figure2_story_icons_manifest.json) · [individual icons](Figure2_metric_icons_README.md)

This **7.5 × 3.19-inch** option removes panel E from the [story layout](Figure2_story_README.md) and uses its width for schematic metric icons. B is grouped more tightly, with a 10 × 20 mm split-half DG reliability icon immediately left of its reliability box plot. C and D each place overlap, Gaussian peak-coverage, and reliable-directed-edge icons immediately left of their corresponding box plots. The overlap icon is 10 × 20 mm; the coverage and edge instances are approximately 6.7 × 20.1 mm. The eight C/D statistical plots retain their source dimensions; their positions move horizontally. A's trajectory tiles are 38 points square, as in the [peak-spread option](Figure2_story_peaks_README.md).

In every icon, the **upper example has the higher raw statistic**, and the arrow points up like the plot y-axis. Colored circles identify DG units, matching Figure 1's blue, red, and yellow plus a fourth purple unit. In overlap, red and yellow denote two distinct units; in reliability, matching colors within each row denote the same unit at $t_1$ and $t_2$. The icons are schematics and contain no measured values or result directions. C and D report paired differences, not the depicted examples: temporal minus constant DG credit in C, and temporal minus constant worker-hit credit in D. B reports learned policy minus random actions. [Individual SVG/PDF/PNG icons](Figure2_metric_icons_README.md) remain available for manual placement or replacement in Overleaf.

## Caption draft

**Policy behavior and temporal credit shape spatial representations.** **A,** NG, UG, and FG trajectory examples from separate terminal 10k-decision frozen-policy probes (seeds 99, 123, and 17), with path efficiency from 100k online windows across five seeds per design. **B,** Frozen-network learned-policy minus random-action differences in DG spatial information and split-half map reliability across 38 terminal models; two paired 50k-decision evaluations are averaged within each model. **C,** Temporal minus mean-matched constant DG credit (push), five matched seeds per design. DG representation readouts use identical observations within each pair, while stored graph edges use separate own-policy snapshots. **D,** Temporal minus constant worker-hit credit (pull), with temporal DG credit fixed, in five matched UG and FG seeds at PPO discount $\gamma=0.999$ and GAE $\lambda=0.995$; representation and graph readouts use separate own-policy snapshots. In B–D, dots are models or matched seed pairs, boxes show quartiles and medians, whiskers show observed extrema, and the horizontal line marks zero. Icons left of the reliability, map-overlap, peak-coverage, and reliable-edge plots illustrate each measure; their upward arrows indicate a higher raw value, not an observed effect. Matching DG-unit colors and $t_1/t_2$ labels distinguish reliability across time from overlap between distinct units; purple is an additional DG-unit color alongside Figure 1's blue, red, and yellow. Map overlap is mean pairwise cosine between active-unit spatial maps. Reliable edges describe stored directed graph structure, not executed goal arrival.
