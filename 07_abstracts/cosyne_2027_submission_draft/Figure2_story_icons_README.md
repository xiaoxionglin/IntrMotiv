This **7.5 × 3.19-inch** option removes panel E from the [story layout](Figure2_story_README.md) and uses its width for schematic metric icons. A has a 10 × 20 mm path-efficiency icon to the left of its dot plot. The plot and three 38-point trajectory tiles move right to accommodate it. Each tile now shows 65 complete sampled trajectory fragments from the same 100k-observation own-policy window protocol, with a shared scale for the local step within each fragment. The figure labels the three architectures NG, UG, and FG without run identifiers. The path-efficiency axis label uses regular weight. B retains a compact grouping with a 10 × 20 mm split-half DG reliability icon immediately left of its reliability box plot. C and D each place overlap, Gaussian peak-coverage, and reliable-directed-edge icons immediately left of their corresponding box plots. Their TS boxes sit lower, with both directed arrows meeting the bottom edges of DG and policy. The overlap icon is 10 × 20 mm; the coverage and edge instances are approximately 6.7 × 20.1 mm. The eight C/D statistical plots retain their source dimensions and positions from the preceding icon variant.

In every icon, the **upper example has the higher raw statistic**, and the arrow points up like the plot y-axis. In A's icon, open and filled circles mark the same start and end in both routes; the dashed chord below shows displacement while the dark detour shows traveled length. In the DG icons, colored circles identify units, matching Figure 1's blue, red, and yellow plus a fourth purple unit. In overlap, red and yellow denote two distinct units; in reliability, red denotes the same unit in both examples at $t_1$ and $t_2$. The icons are schematics and contain no measured values or result directions. C and D report paired differences, not the depicted examples: temporal minus constant DG credit in C, and temporal minus constant worker-hit credit in D. B reports learned policy minus random actions. [Individual SVG/PDF/PNG icons](Figure2_metric_icons_README.md) remain available for manual placement or replacement in Overleaf.

## Caption draft

**Policy behavior and temporal credit shape spatial representations.** **A,** NG, UG, and FG trajectory examples show 65 complete fragments sampled from separate terminal 100k-observation own-policy windows. The color scale gives the step within each fragment, restarting at zero for the next fragment; the examples do not show the full windows. Path efficiency uses the full windows from five runs per design. The schematic compares segment displacement (dashed chord) with traveled length (solid route); the plotted score sums displacement across segments and divides by total length. **B,** Frozen-network learned-policy minus random-action differences in DG spatial information and split-half map reliability across 38 terminal models; two paired 50k-decision evaluations are averaged within each model. **C,** Temporal minus mean-matched constant DG credit (push), five matched seeds per design. DG representation readouts use identical observations within each pair, while stored graph edges use separate own-policy snapshots. **D,** Temporal minus constant worker-hit credit (pull), with temporal DG credit fixed, in five matched UG and FG seeds at PPO discount $\gamma=0.999$ and GAE $\lambda=0.995$; representation and graph readouts use separate own-policy snapshots. In B–D, dots are models or matched seed pairs, boxes show quartiles and medians, whiskers show observed extrema, and the horizontal line marks zero. Icons left of the reliability, map-overlap, peak-coverage, and reliable-edge plots illustrate each measure; their upward arrows indicate a higher raw value, not an observed effect. The reliability icon uses the same red DG unit in both examples, labeled $t_1/t_2$ for the two times; overlap compares distinct red and yellow units. Purple is an additional DG-unit color alongside Figure 1's blue, red, and yellow. Map overlap is mean pairwise cosine between active-unit spatial maps. Reliable edges describe stored directed graph structure, not executed goal arrival.
