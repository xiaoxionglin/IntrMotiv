# All COSYNE terminal DG place-field atlases

These are the **unfiltered complete atlases for all 40 terminal networks** in the main COSYNE C01/C05/C15 field comparison: five seeds (8, 17, 41, 99, 123) in each of eight factor cells. Every PNG displays all 16 DG units, numbered 0–15 in row-major order. No seed or unit was removed because of its field statistics.

## Architecture notes

All three architectures share a fixed ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18 trunk through layer 2, trainable DG projection with batch normalization, fixed CA3-like sequence memory, and learned readout. **C01** explores directly. **C05** navigates toward an internally detected DG target. **C15** adds frontier-based selection of DG targets. The [original diagram](../figures/architecture_v2.svg) shows the common modules.

## Select from every seed

Each cell links the complete 16-unit atlas and states its visited spatial bins out of 361. Select by architecture, DG credit, worker credit, seed, and unit number—for example, `C05 temporal/temporal, seed 123, DG 7`. C01 has no target worker; both C15 rows have the same frontier-target architecture and temporal worker credit.

## What the maps can and cannot show

Each atlas comes from one 10,001-decision policy-driven frozen-model probe near 100 million training frames. Gray bins were unvisited. The response-amplitude color scale is shared by the 16 units **within** an atlas, but differs across atlases. Probe occupancy and sampled trajectories differ across networks; these images are for selecting illustrative units, not for a controlled contrast. Use the [all-seed metric table](full_seed_atlases/terminal_seed_metrics.csv) for exact spatial-information, mono-field, activity, and coverage values. The [per-unit table](full_seed_atlases/terminal_unit_metrics.csv) lists all 640 unit scores, activity fractions, and mono-field measurements. The [source manifest](full_seed_atlases/manifest.json) for remote paths and PNG hashes. The original [checkpoint analysis](../../../06_experiments/cosyne_2027/checkpoint_fields_20261011.md) reports paired statistical results.

The earlier six-image gallery used C01/C05 seed 99 and C15 seed 123 because those example atlases had been copied locally. That gallery was **not an all-seed ranking**. In the original peak illustration, C15 seed 123 was used because constant-credit seed 99 lacked its original terminal checkpoint; a separately indexed recovery later supplied it. Some omitted seeds have stronger statistics: C01 temporal seed 123 has mean DG spatial information 0.217 versus 0.190 for seed 99; C05 temporal/temporal seed 123 visits 312 bins and has a 0.133 strict mono-field fraction versus 190 bins and 0.000 for seed 99. These examples also differ on other measures, so neither seed is universally better.

C15 constant-credit seeds 8 and 99 are recovery runs with a distinct source lineage, recorded in the manifest and the [run catalog](../../../06_experiments/cosyne_2027/training_runs.csv). The atlases were copied from the allocated NEMO2 workspace; no new evaluation or training was run.
