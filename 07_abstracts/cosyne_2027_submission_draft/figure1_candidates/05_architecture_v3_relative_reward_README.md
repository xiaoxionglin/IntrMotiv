## Panel C

Each curve is the five-seed arithmetic mean of intrinsic reward per step at 99 points from 1 to 99 million training frames. For each architecture separately, the renderer divides its mean curve and SEM band by the **median of those 99 plotted means**. The linear vertical axis therefore shows fold of that architecture's own median, and the dotted line marks one fold. This constant scaling preserves the shape of each mean curve and its original SEM band while removing absolute reward units from the comparison. The median is treated as a descriptive scaling constant, so the band is not an uncertainty interval for the normalization factor. The three reward signals are architecture-dependent; curve height here describes within-architecture reward dynamics, not absolute reward magnitude or navigation performance.

Suggested concise caption: **C,** Intrinsic reward relative to each architecture's own median across training, on a linear axis. Lines show five-seed means and shading shows ± SEM after the same within-architecture scaling; the dotted line marks one fold.
