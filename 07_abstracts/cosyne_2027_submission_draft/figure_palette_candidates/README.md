# Figure 1 and Figure 2 palette trials

**Current working selection: H, role-first orange–violet.** Its published files are [Figure 1 SVG](../Figure1.svg) / [PDF](../Figure1.pdf) and [Figure 2 SVG](../Figure2.svg) / [PDF](../Figure2.pdf); the [two-page PDF](review_priority_rgb_orange_violet.pdf) shows them together. Push is orange, pull violet; the DG identities are blue, red, green, and cyan; NG is slate, UG olive, and FG rose. The selection and published paths are recorded in [manifest.json](manifest.json). The original author-edited figure sources and `main.tex` remain as separate layout and manuscript baselines.

The earlier D–F options left UG orange and FG green, which gave those secondary group labels too much of the available hue contrast. The first G–I trial made both groups nearly gray. The revised G–I retain distinct UG and FG hues chosen around each option's push/pull and DG colors: copper/lilac in G, olive/rose in H and I. NG remains slate. Push, pull, and DG identities still determine the primary palette. H supplies the newest standalone figure pair; the other versions remain available for comparison. Its Figure 2 caption must identify the third and fourth illustrative DG units as green and cyan rather than the base figure's yellow and purple. The existing Overleaf ZIP has not been updated.
