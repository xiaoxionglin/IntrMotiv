## Caption draft for panels A–D

**Behavior and temporal credit shape spatial activity.** NG, UG, and FG denote no-goal/direct-exploration (C01), uniform DG-target (C05), and frontier-selected DG-target (C15) agents. Blue, orange, and green identify these designs. **A, behavior:** The red cross marks replacement of the learned policy with random actions during evaluation; network weights remain fixed. Each dot is one terminal model's learned-policy minus random-action value, averaged over two paired 50,000-decision probes. DG spatial information is in bits per decision; split-half spatial-map reliability is measured in readout MLP layer 1. Complete probes are available for 10 NG, 20 UG, and eight FG models. **B, DG credit (push):** Temporal minus mean-matched constant DG encoder credit, with the worker setting fixed; UG retains temporal worker-hit credit. DG information, active-map cosine, and field-peak coverage compare trained encoders on the same 10,001 observations within each matched seed pair. Reliable stored-graph edges come from separate policy-driven 100-million-frame snapshots and have no NG value. There are five matched seeds per displayed design. **C, hit credit (pull):** Temporal minus constant worker-hit credit with temporal DG encoder credit fixed, at PPO discount $\gamma=0.999$ and GAE $\lambda=0.995$. Each UG and FG measure has five paired seeds. DG information, map cosine, field-peak coverage, and stored-graph edges use separate 100,000-decision policy-driven snapshots at 100 million frames. A positive cosine difference means more map overlap; peak coverage is the effective fraction of visited space occupied by eligible dominant field peaks, computed on different visited masks in B and C. In A–C, boxes span the first and third quartiles, center lines are medians, whiskers reach the observed extrema, dots are individual models or seed pairs, and the gray line marks zero. Vertical scales differ across measures; boxes are descriptive, not significance intervals. **D, illustrative DG maps:** Units 4, 7, and 15 from one C15 temporal-credit, seed-123 frozen-policy probe. Gray bins were unvisited, and color uses one within-run scale. These selected examples are separate from the paired ablations.
