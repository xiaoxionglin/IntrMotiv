# Figure 2 story layout: peak-spread sidebar variant

[Editable SVG](Figure2_story_peaks.svg) · [print PDF](Figure2_story_peaks.pdf) · [preview](Figure2_story_peaks.png) · [plotted peaks](Figure2_story_peaks_data.csv) · [source manifest](Figure2_story_peaks_manifest.json)

This **7.5 × 3.19-inch** trial keeps all eleven data plots in A–D. Relative to [the field-map option](Figure2_story_README.md), the three A trajectory tiles are 38 rather than 46 points square, the TS box sits lower in C and D with two directed L-shaped arrows, and the eight C–D statistical components move closer together without changing their size. Panel E uses a **3 × 2 arrangement**: NG, UG, and FG rows; temporal DG credit and constant-credit control columns. Each pair uses the same training seed as its panel-A trajectory example (99, 123, and 17 respectively). In UG, temporal worker-hit credit is fixed in both columns.

Panel E maps use each run's **terminal 100,000-decision online spatial snapshot**. Panel A's trajectory thumbnail is a separate 10,001-decision frozen-policy probe at that checkpoint; its path and occupancy samples are not identical to E's. Each colored dot is one field-eligible DG unit with a dominant peak and at least 0.25% active decisions (15 or 16 peaks per map). Smooth gray Gaussian blurs behind the dots use a shared 100-DMLab-unit width; no spatial-bin grid is drawn. All six maps share world bounds and blur opacity. The number under each map is its effective visited-area fraction, calculated separately from normalized Gaussian density on a pooled 318-bin terminal visited-area mask, then rounded to two decimals. The displayed temporal/control values are NG 0.45/0.24, UG 0.35/0.34, and FG 0.22/0.19. These selected-run values are illustrative, not a seed-level group comparison. The maps also reflect each run's own policy-dependent sampling. [The five-seed cutoff plot](../../06_experiments/results/cosyne_2027_snapshot_peak_spread/figures/gaussian_effective_area_by_cutoff.svg) supplies broader context.

The source peak coordinates, cutoff, mask, Gaussian computation, and source-table hashes are recorded in the [variant manifest](Figure2_story_peaks_manifest.json). The builder reuses the [canonical peak-spread calculation](../../06_experiments/cosyne_2027/analyze_snapshot_peak_spread.py) and verifies every effective-area value against the archived [per-snapshot table](../../06_experiments/results/cosyne_2027_snapshot_peak_spread/per_snapshot_cutoff_spread.csv). To rebuild, run `build_figure2_story.py` first and then `build_figure2_story_peak_variant.py` with the project SF_git Python environment. Refresh the Overleaf ZIP with `build_overleaf_package.py` and optionally `--full`.

## Concise caption for this option

**Policy behavior and temporal credit shape spatial representations.** **A,** NG, UG, and FG trajectory examples from terminal frozen-policy probes (seeds 99, 123, and 17) and path efficiency from separate 100k online windows across five training seeds per design. **B,** Learned-policy minus random-action differences in DG spatial information and split-half map reliability in 38 frozen terminal models. **C,** Temporal versus mean-matched constant DG credit (push) across five matched seeds per design; representation measures use identical observations within each pair. **D,** Temporal versus constant worker-hit credit (pull) with temporal DG credit fixed, across five matched UG and FG seeds under the higher-discount policy setting; representation and graph measures use each policy's own trajectories. In B–D, boxes span quartiles, center lines are medians, whiskers are observed extrema, and dots are models or matched seed pairs. **E,** Same-seed NG, UG, and FG examples contrast temporal DG credit (left) with constant-credit control (right); C05 worker-hit credit remains temporal. Dots mark eligible DG peaks, and gray Gaussian blurs show their spatial spread without a bin grid. Numbers are effective visited-area fractions from Gaussian-smoothed peak density at a 0.25% activity cutoff. Maps use separate terminal 100k online snapshots from A's trajectory probes; they are illustrative own-policy samples, not paired fixed-observation tests.
