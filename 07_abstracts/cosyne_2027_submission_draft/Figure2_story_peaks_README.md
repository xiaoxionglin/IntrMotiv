# Figure 2 story layout: peak-spread sidebar variant

[Editable SVG](Figure2_story_peaks.svg) · [print PDF](Figure2_story_peaks.pdf) · [preview](Figure2_story_peaks.png) · [plotted peaks](Figure2_story_peaks_data.csv) · [source manifest](Figure2_story_peaks_manifest.json)

This **7.5 × 3.19-inch** trial keeps the Figure 2 story layout and all eleven data plots in A–D. Relative to [the field-map option](Figure2_story_README.md), the three A trajectory tiles are 42 rather than 46 points square, the TS box sits lower in C and D with two directed L-shaped arrows, and the eight C–D statistical components shift four points left without changing their size. Panel E uses three 58-point peak-distribution maps from the **same NG, UG, and FG training runs** selected for the A trajectories.

Panel E maps are generated from each run's **separate terminal 100,000-decision online spatial snapshot**. Panel A's trajectory thumbnail is a 10,001-decision frozen-policy probe at that checkpoint; the paths and occupancy samples are not identical. The selected runs are C01 temporal seed 99 (NG), C05 temporal encoder/temporal worker seed 123 (UG), and C15 temporal seed 17 (FG). Each colored dot is one field-eligible DG unit with a dominant peak and at least 0.25% active decisions: 15, 15, and 16 dots respectively. Gray shows equal-mass Gaussian-smoothed peak density with 100-DMLab-unit width. The three maps share world bounds, the pooled 318-bin terminal visited-area mask, and a linear 0–0.04 normalized-mass-per-bin display range; pale gray bins were outside that mask. The number beside each design label is its effective visited-area fraction, rounded from 0.448, 0.346, and 0.225. These three selected-run values are illustrative, not a seed-level group comparison. [The five-seed cutoff plot](../../06_experiments/results/cosyne_2027_snapshot_peak_spread/figures/gaussian_effective_area_by_cutoff.svg) supplies that broader context.

The source peak coordinates, cutoff, mask, Gaussian computation, source-table hashes, and display scale are recorded in the [variant manifest](Figure2_story_peaks_manifest.json). The builder reuses the [canonical peak-spread calculation](../../06_experiments/cosyne_2027/analyze_snapshot_peak_spread.py) and verifies every effective-area value against the archived [per-snapshot table](../../06_experiments/results/cosyne_2027_snapshot_peak_spread/per_snapshot_cutoff_spread.csv). To rebuild, run `build_figure2_story.py` first and then `build_figure2_story_peak_variant.py` with the project SF_git Python environment. Refresh the Overleaf ZIP with `build_overleaf_package.py` and optionally `--full`.

## Concise caption for this option

**Policy behavior and temporal credit shape spatial representations.** **A,** NG, UG, and FG trajectory examples from terminal frozen-policy probes (seeds 99, 123, and 17) and path efficiency from separate 100k online windows across five training seeds per design. **B,** Learned-policy minus random-action differences in DG spatial information and split-half map reliability in 38 frozen terminal models. **C,** Temporal versus mean-matched constant DG credit (push) across five matched seeds per design; representation measures use identical observations within each pair. **D,** Temporal versus constant worker-hit credit (pull) with temporal DG credit fixed, across five matched UG and FG seeds under the higher-discount policy setting; representation and graph measures use each policy's own trajectories. In B–D, boxes span quartiles, center lines are medians, whiskers are observed extrema, and dots are models or matched seed pairs. **E,** Eligible DG peak locations (colored dots) and normalized Gaussian peak density (gray) for the same three *training runs* as A, measured in separate terminal 100k online snapshots. All maps share world bounds, an empirical pooled visited-area mask, and a linear 0–0.04 density scale. Numbers give effective visited-area fractions at a 0.25% activity cutoff; the maps are illustrative single-run examples.
