This 6.78 × 4.49 inch layout keeps the author's [Figure2_fiddle.svg](Figure2_fiddle.svg) plots and diagram groups in place. The lower strip adds three maps from the **C15 temporal DG-credit, seed-123** terminal network linked in the [complete atlas index](figure1_candidates/full_place_field_atlases.md): DG 4, 7, and 15. They come from one 10,001-decision frozen-policy probe near 100 million training frames, which visited 307 of 361 spatial bins. Gray means unvisited; color uses the source atlas's shared **within-run** scale. DG 4 meets the archived strict single-component criterion; DG 7 and 15 are informative spatial responses but should not be described as single compact fields. Selection is illustrative, not an estimate of prevalence. The dashed cards reserve space for split-half reliability, map-overlap, peak-coverage, and graph-edge illustrations; they contain no data.

The PDF now has a nonnegative view box so its top row is not clipped on export. The incomplete source axis label is shown as “Δ reliability”; the caption defines its split-half measure.

The constant-credit C15 seed-123 [atlas](figure1_candidates/full_seed_atlases/c15_constant_s123.png) is available for a later visual comparison. That model follows a different policy-driven trajectory and visits 239 of 361 bins, so juxtaposed maps would illustrate two separate probes rather than a fixed-input credit effect. The paired replay boxes above are the controlled representation comparison.

## Caption draft for this layout

**Behavior and temporal credit shape spatial activity.** NG, UG, and FG are no-goal/direct-exploration (C01), uniform DG-target (C05), and frontier-selected DG-target (C15) agents. Blue, orange, and green identify these designs. **Top:** With network weights fixed, learned-policy values minus random-action values are shown for DG spatial information (bits per decision) and readout MLP layer-1 split-half spatial-map reliability. Each dot is a terminal model, averaged over two paired 50,000-decision probes; 10 NG, 20 UG, and eight FG models have complete evaluations. **Middle:** Temporal minus mean-matched constant DG encoder credit with the worker setting fixed; UG retains temporal worker-hit credit. The first three measures compare terminal networks on the same 10,001 observations within each matched seed pair. Reliable graph edges come from separate 100-million-frame policy-driven snapshots and have no NG value. There are five paired seeds per displayed design. **Bottom:** Temporal minus constant worker-hit credit with temporal DG credit fixed, at PPO discount $\gamma=0.999$ and GAE $\lambda=0.995$. Each UG and FG measure has five paired seeds; representation and graph measures use separate 100,000-decision policy-driven snapshots at 100 million frames. Positive map-overlap values mean more similar DG response maps; field-peak coverage measures the effective fraction of visited space covered by eligible dominant peaks, so its mask differs between the middle and bottom protocols. Across all plots, boxes span quartiles, center lines are medians, whiskers show the observed range, dots show individual models or seed pairs, and zero is the no-change reference. Vertical scales differ by measure, and the boxes are descriptive rather than significance intervals. **Maps:** Three selected DG responses from the C15 temporal-credit seed-123 terminal network (units 4, 7, and 15) in one frozen-policy probe, with a shared within-run color scale and gray unvisited bins. The map examples are separate from the paired ablations. Dashed cards mark proposed, non-data illustrations.
